Delete your Playr account

Playr app by Cogni GenNxt Pvt Ltd

You can ask us to delete your Playr account and the data linked to it. You don't need to have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email tharun@cognigennxt.com with the subject "Playr account deletion request". Send it from the email address on your account, if you have one.
  2. Include your Playr username and the mobile number registered on your account.
  3. We may contact you to confirm that the account is yours. We will never ask for your password or OTP.
  4. We will delete your account within 30 days of confirming your request, and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, username, mobile number, email address, photo, bio, date of birth, gender, address and location.
  • Your sports preferences and tournament registrations.
  • Your posts and tournaments, with their photos and videos.
  • Your comments, reactions, saved items, follower and following connections, and chat messages.
  • Your device identifiers and notification tokens.

What we may keep

  • Records we must keep to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or investigate misuse, for as long as the law requires or those matters remain open.
  • Reports about content or conduct that other users submitted, with your personal details removed where possible.
  • Copies in routine backups, until those backups expire.

Deletion is permanent. You won't be able to recover your account or content afterwards. For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
