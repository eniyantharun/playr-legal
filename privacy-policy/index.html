Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Playr is a sports community app operated by Cogni GenNxt Pvt Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Playr mobile app (package com.cognigennxt.playr), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, username, mobile number, email address and sign-in details, including basic Google account information if you use Google Sign-In.
  • Profile details: profile photo, bio, date of birth, gender, address, PIN code and account type.
  • Sports details: sports preferences, player details, education details you choose to add, and tournaments you create or join.
  • Content you share: posts, photos, videos, comments, reactions, saved items and tags.
  • Messages: chat messages you send and receive in the app.
  • Reports and support: reports you submit about posts or events, and messages you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: your device location, while you use the app and only if you give permission. We use it to show nearby tournaments, posts and players.
  • Device information: device model, device identifiers and the push notification token for your device.
  • Activity: account activity such as followers, following and when your account was created.

Device permissions

The app asks for access to your camera and photos so you can add images, your location for nearby content, and notifications for alerts. You can turn these off at any time in your device settings, although related features may stop working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and verify you, including one-time passwords (OTPs).
  • To run the app's features: profiles, posts, tournaments, followers, chat and nearby content.
  • To send notifications about activity, such as follower updates, tags and tournaments.
  • To review reports, moderate content and keep the community safe.
  • To respond to support requests, maintain and improve the app, and meet legal obligations.

3. What other users can see

Your profile details, posts, comments and tournaments may be visible to other Playr users. Please think carefully about what you share.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the app, including Amazon Web Services (hosting, database and file storage) and Google/Firebase (sign-in, chat and push notifications), and providers that deliver verification messages.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets.
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

5. Storage and security

Your information is stored on cloud servers, including in India. Our service providers may process it in other countries. We encrypt data in transit and take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your information as described on our account deletion page, except where we must keep it for legal reasons.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile details in the app.
  • You can delete your own posts and tournaments in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data.
  • You can email us to request access to or correction of your information, or to object to how we use it.

8. Children

Playr is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
